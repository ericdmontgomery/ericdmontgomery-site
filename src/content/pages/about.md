About

# Eric D. Montgomery

The literature on this site is meant for entertainment. Here, you’ll find stories from a variety of genres, tales stylized for rabbit holes that begin with the mundane and delve into the tantalizing worlds of what could be.

Eric D. Montgomery is a former Californian currently exploring Thailand. His fascination with Anthropology permeates through his imagined landscapes as he brings to life the pacifist tribes of the Jai-Nar nation, the sleepy southern town of Sweetwater, and the lost souls that wander the Steppes of Moa. These character-driven plots peel away the accepted answers to expose intricate layers of reality beneath.

*“Let others wonder whether or not the tree makes a sound. I’d rather know how it fell, and even what will happen next.”*
